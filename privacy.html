Privacy Policy — the Ghar website

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version: This page covers the Ghar website (gharlearn.com). The website has parent accounts so a child's learning can follow them across devices. We collect as little as we can: a parent's email address for sign-in, a first name or nickname for each child, and the child's learning progress. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The Ghar iOS app works differently and has its own privacy policy.

Parent accounts

A parent signs in with an email address, or with Google. We email a short sign-in code (or a sign-in link) — there is no password. If a parent chooses Google, Google shares the email address of their Google account with us so we can sign them in; we use it only for that, exactly as if they had typed it in directly. Your email address is used only to sign you in: we send no marketing email to it, and we never sell it or share it for marketing. Apart from your email address and when the account was created, we store nothing else about the parent.

Child profiles

A parent creates a profile for each child using a first name or a nickname — please don't use a child's full name. We never ask children for an email address or any contact information, and accounts belong to parents, not children.

What we store for each child

So that progress follows the child across devices, our server stores their learning progress: lessons heard and completed, XP, streaks, missions, quiz and game results, and shop and My Ghar items. This data is stored on the servers that run Ghar and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Mission "Prove it" recordings are different: they are kept only in the browser's storage on the device that made them, so the family can listen back on that device. They are never sent to or stored on our servers.

Leaderboards (optional)

If a player joins a leaderboard group, we store the nickname they pick, the code of the group, and their scores: total XP, best quiz streak, total correct quiz answers, and missions completed. Nicknames and scores are visible to other members of the same group. A nickname does not have to be a real name — please use a fun nickname rather than a real name.

Friend games (optional)

Players who share a leaderboard group can challenge each other to one-on-one games — chess, Bagh-Chal, and short quiz duels. To run those games we store the moves made, the scores and finishing times, and who won. A player's nickname, moves and results in a game are visible to the other player in that game, and each child's friend games are listed in the parent dashboard, so parents can see who their child has played and how the games went. Friend games have no chat and no way to send messages or share personal information — the only thing exchanged is the game itself.

Ask Bhunte — our AI helper

A signed-in learner can ask Bhunte questions about Nepali words and lessons. Bhunte is an AI helper, powered by an AI service. When a learner asks a question, we send the AI service only the question itself and the relevant lesson material from Ghar. We never send the child's name, the parent's email address, or any account identifier, and questions and answers are not used to train AI models.

Questions and Bhunte's answers are stored in the family's account on our server so that parents can read them at any time in the parent dashboard. Bhunte is designed to answer only Nepali-learning questions and to never ask a child for personal information. A daily question limit applies.

Guests

You can use the website without signing in. In that case, progress is kept only in that browser's storage on that device and is not sent to us — apart from the leaderboard, if the player joins a group.

Sessions and tracking

When a parent signs in, the browser keeps a session token in its local storage so the parent stays signed in; it is not a tracking cookie. The website has no advertising, no analytics services, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not sell, rent, or share information with third parties for marketing. To run the website we use a few trusted service providers — for hosting, for delivering sign-in email, and for the AI behind Ask Bhunte. They process the information described on this page only to provide those services to us, never for their own purposes.

Deleting website data

A parent can delete a child's profile, or the whole account, at any time from the parent dashboard. Deleting a profile permanently removes that child's progress, leaderboard identity, friend games, and Ask Bhunte history from our server. Deleting the account also removes the parent's email address. You can also ask us to delete data by emailing the contact below.

Purchases on the website

The website does not currently take payments. If that changes, we will update this policy before it does.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
